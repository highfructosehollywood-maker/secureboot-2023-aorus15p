How far these were verified
---------------------------
The 0.2.3b build above was run once, and the stick it produced was booted on the target
machine: the Security page appears and SREP.log reports the patch applied. Four of the
eight files are unchanged from the previous release and came out byte-identical to the
hashes published for it - EFI/BOOT/BOOTX64.EFI, Oniguruma.efi, README.txt and
SREP_Config.cfg - which is the reproducibility of the container demonstrated across
separate runs on different source trees. The driver, the archive, and the two files that
carry its filename (startup.nsh, LICENSES.txt) have not yet been rebuilt a second time.
